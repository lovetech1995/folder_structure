🗣️ DEBATE CARD #{number}

📌 CHỦ ĐỀ: {one-line topic}

─── PROPOSE (AI) ──────────────────────────────────────
Giải pháp: {what I want to do}
Lý do:
• {reason 1} (từ {source})
• {reason 2} (từ {source})
Alternative:

• Option B: {option} — {pros/cons}
Cost impact:
• Reads: {X} → {Y} • Writes: {X} → {Y}
• Monthly: ~${X}

─── CHALLENGE (Human) ─────────────────────────────────
{questions / concerns / counter-proposals}

─── DEFEND / REVISE (AI) ─────────────────────────────
{response / revised proposal}

─── RESOLUTION ───────────────────────────────────────
✅ APPROVED  ── Ghi chú: {notes}
❌ REJECTED  ── Lý do: {reason}
🔄 REVISE    ── Yêu cầu: {what to change}
⏸️ DEFER     ── Ghi backlog #{id}
